Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a Power BI dashboard?

Most single-function dashboards (sales, inventory, or financial reporting) take two to four weeks from discovery to handover, depending on the complexity of your data sources and the number of KPIs required. Multi-function executive dashboards typically take four to six weeks.

Can you connect Power BI to our existing ERP or CRM system?

Yes. Power BI supports over 100 native connectors, and we have experience integrating with common ERP, CRM, and accounting platforms. If your system requires a custom connection, we can typically build one using SQL or API-based integration.

Do we need to migrate away from Excel to use Power BI?

No. Power BI can connect directly to your existing Excel files as a data source, and many of our clients use both tools together — Excel for ad hoc analysis and financial modelling, Power BI for automated, multi-user dashboard reporting.

Will our team be able to maintain the dashboard after handover?

Yes. We document every data model, DAX measure, and configuration step, and provide a walkthrough session so your team understands how the dashboard works and how to make minor updates going forward.

Can you build dashboards for multiple departments at once?

Yes. We frequently build connected dashboard suites — for example, a sales dashboard, an inventory dashboard, and an executive summary dashboard that consolidates KPIs from both — all built on a shared, governed data model.
